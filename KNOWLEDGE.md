# Knowledge Base

Durable learnings for building in Pigment via the MCP connector. Add to it
proactively whenever something non-obvious is learned — a tool behaving
unexpectedly, a formula pattern that works (or fails), a Pigment constraint.

Each entry: a short title, the fact, why it matters, and the source.

---

## Connector behavior

**Some operations aren't exposed as MCP tools.** Depending on your connector's tool
set, you may not be able to create views from scratch, rename blocks after creation,
or move blocks between folders. Plan to create objects in the right folder, with the
right name, on the first try — and record any gaps you hit here.
_Source: observed connector behavior — verify against your own connector's tool list._
